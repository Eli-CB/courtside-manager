# The Draft tab: js/draft.js

Part of the repo's notes: CLAUDE.md says which file covers what and how to keep them current. Read this before changing js/draft.js or keeper detection.

- draft: Draft tab/board. Draft Retrospective (was Draft awards) at the bottom (`draftAwards`, this season only: today's ADP says nothing about a past draft; shared as the Recap's standouts): each pick against consensus ADP (`adpOf`: a Fantrax league's own, else the daily file's, kept as `raw.adp` for ESPN/Sleeper), the league's keepers off the board first (a player's consensus spot is his place in ADP order among the players not kept: in a keeper league comparing raw pick numbers made the typical pick a 20-spot reach). Reach (was The Reach): furthest ahead of his spot; Fall (was The Fall): furthest past it (Value Meal, the team whose typical pick went latest, was dropped, the owner's ask, Nov 2026). An award's title opens its breakdown (`detail`, `draftAwardDetails`, 'draft-award'). From the players themselves (no ADP needed): Glass (games missed a season the last two seasons: `SPORT.seasonGames` less his games from the season archives, `glassSeasons`/`missedSeasons`; a season before he was in the league, by `raw.exp`, isn't his, one he sat out whole is all missed; goalies left out; its card: the one who missed most), Youngins / Uncs (average age on draft day, `born`), Homer (was The Homer: most picks from one real team, by each player's team now; its card names them and shows their faces, a tie each team's, the first taken of each as its face) / Passport (was The Globalist, then Frequent Flyer: most countries, `raw.nat`; its card: each winner's first player taken from outside the league's most common country, `countryName`). Trigger Finger / Clock Milker: each team's average pick time (the owner's ask, Nov 2026: one number, the median beside it confused), its card naming the winner's quickest / longest pick with his face, as Youngins / Uncs its youngest / oldest player (`standout` in `draftAwardKit`), from Fantrax's pick times (`league.draft.picks[i][5]`, `league.draft.start`, written by js/fantrax.js and, for the site's own league, by update_data.py (`fx_ms`): the page uses the daily file's copy of that league; a pick's time is the wait since the one before; a draft whose picks share one time, as Fantrax rewrites old drafts, has none; ESPN/Sleeper/Yahoo give none). Fantrax has no pick-clock length or auto-pick flag (Sleeper has the clock, ESPN marks auto-picks: `autoDraftTypeId`). Glass, Youngins, Uncs, Homer and Passport count each team's keepers with its picks (its draft-day roster); the ADP and pick-time awards are picks only; team awards need half the most players; a tie names each team. No emoji on any award, and the Draft tab draws them as the Recap's cards (`standoutCard`, recap.js; the owner's asks, Nov 2026), and every team award names the winner's standout player with his face on its card (`standout`; the owner's ask, Nov 2026). Drafts are graded on the games of the league's own season
  (`leagueSeason`: first week to last, playoffs included; `draftSeason`/`windowStats` from that season's game log), not
  the NBA's full season: past seasons always, this season once the league is over or if it started late.

## Rules settled with the owner (keep them)
- Keepers exist only if draft rounds < roster spots (starters + bench); keepers = opening-week roster minus
  that team's picks. Keepers are graded as the #5 and #15 picks: this season the better one by ADP, a past season by
  the season before's value per game (`L.priorSeason` archive); never compare today's ADP to a past draft.
