# Trades: js/views.js

Part of the repo's notes: CLAUDE.md says which file covers what and how to keep them current. Read this before changing Trades in js/views.js.

- Trades' Trade proposal: one card, one table under the lists (`tradeCard`, from `tradeModel` and, for your own deal, `tradeForYou`; the owner's ask, Nov 2026: no whitespace, all in one section): Share and an (i) on top (`INFO.trade` says what every column means: the owner's ask, the table carries no notes, no value line, and no labels the layout already says, like Player, Per game or Total; one header row: GP, Missed, Playoff G, MIN...); no verdict, never "you win" or "roughly even" (the owner's rule: whether a trade is fair is for the people trading to judge); for each side its players (photo, team, colored positions, GP and Missed this season: `seasonGp`/`gamesMissed` in nba.js, his team's games before the data's day less his, none for a goalie; playoff games, the per-game line, value; no ranks, the owner removed them), then its total, the better side's marked (a side getting one player has no total row: his row is it, the owner's ask) (green/red only when one side is yours; Value added up with "over waiver" under it, which is what's marked; FG%/FT% show makes-attempts and are marked by makes over the league average on those attempts, not the bare %, the owner's ask: volume matters); last For your team (your active roster's per-game change). Playoff games (the owner's ask: they decide titles): his NBA team's games in the league's playoff periods (`playoffDays`/`playoffGames` in nba.js: periods from `L.playoffPeriod` on, by `periodDates`; only those still to play once they've started; a goalie's expected starts; Sleeper's one-game weeks count weeks); no column without playoffs. A points league's FP is the Value column. Lists show team and positions, not the next game or an injury note (the owner's asks). Future draft picks (the owner's ask, Nov 2026): chips under each list (`leaguePicks`, fantrax.js: read the first time Trades needs them, kept on the league object and its saved copy, so read again only with the league; not via changed('league'), which Sleeper leagues hear on game nights), in the deal as `givePicks`/`getPicks` (`pickKey` year:round:original team; link `&givepicks=&getpicks=`), shown on the getting side's rows, the picture and the text, never valued (next season's order isn't known). Fantrax: `getDraftPicks` futureDraftPicks (checked: your league lists 2027 only, 11 rounds; the points test league answers none; its currentDraftPicks are this draft's, an unmade pick included, not read). Sleeper: `traded_picks` lists only traded ones; the seasons (3 in dynasty, 1 in keeper, none in redraft) are an assumption: the owner's dynasty league has traded 2027 and 2028 picks, none of 2029 yet. ESPN has no pick trading. Share as the Recap's (`tradeShare`, picture `tradeCanvas`, ranks there too), link `?league=&from=&to=&give=&get=#trade` (`tradeUrl`): either team gets the deal in its own pickers, from its side; anyone else sees it as sent, above theirs (`applyTradeLink`, `ui.tradeSeen`).
